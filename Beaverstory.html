Five narrative tenses, five jobs

Past simple
A completed action or fact at a definite point in the past. "…the last wild beaver in England disappeared."
Past continuous
An action in progress, often interrupted by another action. "…while conservationists were surveying wildlife… they noticed something impossible."
Past perfect
An action completed before another point in the past. "…hunters had hunted them for so long that, by 1526, the last beaver disappeared."
Present perfect simple
A past action with a result or relevance now, or an unfinished state. "The beavers' dams have reduced flooding in nearby villages."
Present perfect continuous
An ongoing action, with emphasis on its duration up to now. "Scientists have been studying the beavers… since that first sighting."
